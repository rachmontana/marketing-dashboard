MARKETING SUMMARY & ANALYSIS - JUNE 2026 | Week 2 of 4 (11/22 business days - 50% through month)

OVERALL STANDING

June is at the halfway point and the funnel is under significant stress below the lead line. Leads are running at 91% of goal - the one bright spot - but everything downstream is behind pace. SQLs are at 32% of goal (180 vs. 556), Total Opps at 48% (135 vs. 283), and SQOs at 38% (69 vs. 184). Marketing Sourced ACV is at $108,564 against a $183,333 goal (59%), though at 50% of the month elapsed the ACV pace is slightly behind. The pattern from May is repeating and accelerating: lead volume is there, but conversion from lead to SQL to opp is breaking down severely.

THE CORE PROBLEM: THE FUNNEL IS BREAKING AT SQL

The data tells a very clear story. Leads are at 91% of June goal. But SQLs are at only 32%. That is not a lead quality problem in isolation - it is a qualification and handoff breakdown. Something is preventing leads from being worked and qualified in time. Whether that is an MQL routing issue, a lead scoring problem (Bernadette has a rock specifically for lead scoring refinements), or MDR capacity constraints, this is the single most important thing to diagnose this week. Every unqualified lead that ages out is lost pipeline.

The Opp-to-SQO conversion rate is actually strong - 75% in June vs. a 70% goal - which means once opps are created, the team is converting them well. The problem is that not enough opps are being created in the first place because the SQL→Opp step is choking.

COHORT BREAKDOWN = JUNE MTD

Outperformer - Lacerte/ProSeries

The only large-volume cohort tracking well. $45,786 ACV against a $44,821 goal - already at 102% with half the month left. Opps (42%) and SQOs (59%) are behind goal but won deal conversion is strong (12 of 19 goal). This cohort has momentum, let's make sure nothing interrupts it.

Surprise outperformer - CCH

$24,300 ACV against a $5,540 goal (439%). CCH is massively overperforming again. Small goal, big result. Similar story to May. To try and squeeze more out of this cohort, Bernadette is sending an ad hoc email to CCH users this month.

Critical underperformers:

General Accounting - 28 opps vs. 93 goal (30%), 9 SQOs vs. 56 goal (16%), 3 won deals vs. 20 goal (15%), $10,260 ACV vs. $39,941 goal (26%). This is the same problem as May, now in month two. This is a structural issue, not a one-month anomaly. General Accounting leads are coming in (Zoominfo and Events are top sources) but they are not converting. This needs a dedicated intervention.

ProConnect - $2,400 ACV against a $26,701 goal (9%). Only 1 won deal through half the month. This is a dramatic reversal from May when ProConnect was at 171% of goal. Something changed. This is urgent.

Drake - 0 won deals, $0 ACV vs. $5,168 goal. Zero. An ad hoc email is being sent to this cohort this month as well.

UltraTax CS - Opps (124%) and SQOs (121%) are both ahead of goal, which is genuinely good pipeline news. But won deals are only 9 of 13 goal (69%) and ACV is at 63%. The pipeline is there - the close rate needs work. This is the most actionable near-term opportunity: deals are in flight, they just need to close.

MDR TEAM - JUNE MTD

Total MDR opps are at 66 through 11 days - if sustained, that's on track for a strong month. Bryan (13), India (20), Javier (10), Kenny (14). India is having her best month since January. Bryan is running at roughly the same weekly pace as his strong May. The MDR team is one of the few channels actually performing. The Outreach Amplify pilot and LinkedIn MDR Outbound Activation rocks (Bryan's) should accelerate this.

RECOMMENDED ACTIONS FOR THIS WEEK

1. Emergency SQL diagnostic (this week, highest priority)

SQLs are at 32% of goal halfway through the month. Pull every MQL created in June and trace its status: how many have been contacted, how many have been disqualified and why, and how many are sitting untouched. If leads are aging unworked, that is a capacity or routing problem that can be fixed immediately. If they're being disqualified at high rates, the lead scoring model needs to be revisited - which is already a rock for Bernadette.

2. ProConnect intervention

May to June: from 171% of ACV goal to 9%. Something changed dramatically. Is it a campaign that ended? Do we need a CPAA webinar turned on this month for this cohort? ProConnect has 16 opps in the funnel but only 1 won deal. There are deals to be closed - someone needs to own accelerating them.

3. UltraTax CS close push

UltraTax CS has 47 SQOs through half the month - strong pipeline. With 4 won deals needed to hit goal, focus Sales on advancing in-flight UTCS deals this week. This is the clearest near-term ACV opportunity.

4. General Accounting — escalate beyond marketing

Two months of the same pattern. 30% opp conversion, 16% SQO conversion, 15% close rate. Marketing can keep generating leads but if they don't convert, volume is wasted. This conversation needs to happen with Sales leadership this week. Are General Accounting prospects being treated as a different buyer type? Do they need a different qualification script? Is there a pricing or product fit issue?

5. SmartRequestAI pipeline emergency

43 opps through May against a 1,302 season goal means this rock needs ~130 opps/month for the remaining four months. Blake's SRAI summer content rock and Chad's rock to exceed 275 SRAI opps for the season are directly relevant. What is the specific campaign plan to drive SRAI opps in June?

6. Lead source diversification

Zoominfo (2,136 leads in May) is the top source by a wide margin, but these leads are converting to SQLs at a very low rate - which may explain the SQL gap. If Zoominfo leads are lower intent, the team should evaluate whether lead scoring thresholds for this source need adjustment, and whether Event and Organic leads (which are likely higher intent) are being prioritized in the MDR queue.
